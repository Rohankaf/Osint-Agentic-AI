️ Tools Used in OSINT Pipeline

 theHarvester

Purpose:  
Used for gathering emails, hostnames, and subdomains from public sources.

Key Capabilities:
-  Email Harvesting
-  Subdomain Discovery
-  Hostnames & DNS names
-  Source Tracking (Bing, Hunter, etc.)
-  Output Formats: JSON, XML, HTML

When to Use:  
Use with domains (e.g., openai.com) for discovering emails and infrastructure.
